# EPISODE ONE — THE COIN LANDS
## E1 — BALDY THE MAGNIFICENT

**NORTH BLUE — COIN TOSS ISLAND. THE PIT, GULLWHARF. PIT NIGHT.**

The Pit is packed, and Merdou is losing.

Not to anybody good. To Brakko the Anvil — chain-wrapped fists, four teeth, a man who has never
had two thoughts in the same evening. Brakko hits Merdou in the face. Merdou takes it, because he
is not looking at Brakko. He is looking at a dye-girl from Petal Row, third row up, and waving.

"OI! MISS! DO YOU LIKE FIGHTERS OR WINNERS? I CAN BE EITHER!"

Brakko hits him again. The whiskey in Merdou's fist slops over his knuckles.

"Baldy's down two rounds!" the announcer yells from the rail. "Odds are moving! Odds are — wait,
nobody's betting on Baldy. Odds are staying exactly where they are!"

In the second row, a grandmother with a coin purse in her lap counts out five coppers, thinks
better of it, and puts three back. Two bookies fight over her business anyway. Behind them the
odds board shows BRAKKO in fat chalk and, under CHALLENGER, someone has just written *why*.

Pit night at Gullwharf. Half the district is here — dye-girls off shift, wharfmen still in their
boots, kids who are absolutely not allowed to be here watching through the fence slats and doing
the commentary themselves.

"THE MAGNIFICENT MERDOU," Merdou roars at the crowd, "IS PACING HIMSELF!"

At the rail, a lean man in a high-collared coat sips his drink. Two stray cats sit on the rail
beside him like they bought tickets.

"You're losing to Brakko," Ashren says.

"I'm WARMING UP."

"You said that last month. Then you slept in the ring."

"THAT WAS TRAINING."

Up in the owner's box, a goon lowers a spyglass and whispers to the biggest thing on the island.
Yuri Gups — elephant Mink, small spectacles, silk cloth in one hand — stops dabbing at his ledger.

"Sober?" says Gups. "You're certain. Tonight of all nights."

"Mostly sober, General."

"Cancel my exhibition." He draws one neat line through a page. "The old injury is acting up."

The two goons look at each other and know better than to have faces about it.

Down in the sand, Brakko swings, and this time Merdou leans back and the fist takes the whiskey
glass clean out of his hand. It breaks on the pit wall. The crowd goes "ohhhh." The dye-girl
laughs.

Merdou looks at his empty hand.

"That was FULL," he says.

"Uh," says Brakko.

"That was a FULL GLASS, you CHAIN-HANDED COW."

Brakko backs up one step. The grandmother in the second row stops counting her coins. The
bookies stop shouting numbers. Everyone on Coin Toss Island knows what an angry Archon sounds
like, even if nobody knows what an Archon is.

And Brakko — because he has never had two thoughts in the same evening — reaches into his
waistband wrap and pulls out a foot of sharpened steel. In a fists-only pit. The boos start at
the top bleachers and come down row by row. One of the fence-slat kids boos loudest of all.

Merdou rolls his neck. Sets his feet in the sand.

"UHUHUHU!! Now THAT'S entertainment."
