# E3 — FUCK IT

**THE SIX HUNDRED STEPS. VERY LATE. VERY DRUNK.**

The Steps go up forever and the two of them are taking the scenic route, which is the same route,
but slower and with more arguing.

"Six hundred steps," Merdou announces, "and not ONE of them even. This island was built by drunks."

"It was built by a legion."

"Drunk legion."

They climb. Somewhere below, Gullwharf is putting itself to bed. A dog has been following Ashren
since the Kettle. It is carrying his glove and he has stopped fighting it.

"So," Ashren says. "Joro."

"What about him."

"You did something about it." A beat. "You haven't done something about anything in seven years."

"I do PLENTY."

"You drink, you brawl, you fail with women in ways I collect like seashells—"

"UHUHUHU—"

"—and every man in that tavern is more of a coward than you, and none of them are wasting
anything, because none of them have anything to waste." Ashren isn't smiling now. "You're the
only man on this island who could've been something, and you've spent seven years making sure
nobody finds out. And that's the one joke I've never once found funny."

"Careful, bird."

"Or WHAT, Merdou? What's the punishment? You'll be a disappointment AT me? Get in line behind—"

"I KILLED MY FATHER."

The Steps are very quiet.

The dog puts the glove down.

"Seven years ago," Merdou says. Not loud now. Loud is gone. "You want to know what I was. I was
the son who wasn't good enough. And I killed him, and I took what I took, and I ran here, and I
drank. That's the something I did. That's the last something I did."

Ashren looks at him for a long time.

Then he takes off his coat.

It takes Merdou's whiskey-soaked brain a second to understand what he's looking at, because what
he's looking at is wings. Opened all the way for the first time since he's known him — dark, and
scarred along one edge, and big enough to catch the wind that comes up the cliff.

"They burned my whole race," Ashren says. "The World Government. Took my parent. I was a kid. I
have been hiding in coats my entire life, on an island where nobody looks up." He flexes the
wings once, like a fist unclenching. "You told me your worst thing. There's mine. And here's the
part I've never said drunk or sober: I'm going to bring them back. The Lunarians. That's my
something. I've been waiting seven years to start it, and I've been waiting on YOU, you enormous
bald disaster, because I watched a drunk man laugh through the worst beating I ever gave anyone,
and I thought: that one. That one's got a king in him somewhere, if he'd stop drowning it."

The wind comes up the Steps. The dog sneezes.

"...Fuck it," Merdou says.

"Fuck it?"

"FUCK IT. I'm done rotting. You're done hiding. We do something with our lives. Starting—" he
checks the sky, finds dawn distressingly close, "—starting after we sleep. Then we do something.
Something BIG."

"The champion," Ashren says. "The pits. The Toll. All of it — that's the something. It's been in
front of us the whole time."

"Gups." Merdou says the name like a toast. "UHUHUHU. Oh, he is going to HATE this."

Ashren folds the wings away, and for once his grin isn't the performed one.

"To the tribe, then," he says, and holds out the bottle. "And whatever you're about to swear to
it."
