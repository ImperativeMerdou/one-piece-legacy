# E5 — CHAMPION'S RULES

**THE COLOSSEUM. FIGHT DAY. FULL HOUSE.**

**ONE WEEK LATER.**

«IF C4 = 1» Seven days of Ashren's camp and Merdou walks the fighters' tunnel feeling like a
weapon someone finally cleaned. He has hated every single day of it. Loudly.
«IF C4 = 2» Seven days in ledgers and back rooms and Merdou walks the tunnel with bags under his
eyes and a folded page of Priya's handwriting inside his shirt like a knife.
«IF C4 = 3» The betting window took everything they own and the whole island knows it. Merdou
walks the tunnel broke, famous, and delighted with himself.

The Colosseum is the old Legion assembly with eleven years of gold paint on it. Forty rows.
Petal-dye banners. The great bronze bell hanging over the champion's gate. The floor is a
copper-inlaid mosaic — waves and coins and a trunk-raised champion — polished to a shine.

At the mouth of the sand, an official waits with a silver cup on a cushion.

"The ceremonial waters," he announces. "Every challenger drinks. Champion's rules."

Merdou looks into the cup.

Merdou looks closer into the cup.

"There's a THUMBPRINT on the rim," he says, with more horror than he showed the knife. "This cup
is FILTHY. Who WASHES these?! Do you wash ANYTHING?! Get it away from me before I catch whatever
the last man had."

The official's smile dies slowly, in stages. Up in the champion's box, something very large
pinches the bridge of its trunk.

«IF C4 = 2» It wouldn't have mattered — the folded page in Merdou's shirt has the "ceremonial
waters" itemized under *fight-day expenses*, right beneath the referee's bonus. Some things you
break with a week's digging. Some things break on a dirty cup.

"FIRST," the announcer bellows, "THE UNDERCARD GAUNTLET! CHAMPION'S RULES! THE CHALLENGERS WILL
FACE THE CROWN'S FINEST BEFORE EARNING THE CHAMPION!"

The Crown's finest file out. Brakko — arm splinted or not, depending on the month he's had — and
five friends who make the sand look small.

Ashren hands his coat to a cat.

"The Persuader works alone on the left," he says, unhooking the flail. "Try not to fall on me."

The gauntlet is six men who were promised an easy afternoon. The flail starts counting.

"METRONOME." One-two. One-two. The first goon's guard comes apart at the seams like wet paper.
The crowd, which came for a fix and is getting a fight, starts making a very different sound.

But six is six, and champion's rules mean the referee is blind on one side, and somewhere in the
third exchange the afternoon stops being easy for everyone. There is a man with a hammer. There
is a man with a net weighted with hooks — pit-illegal for twenty years, invisible to today's
referee. The biggest of them hasn't moved yet. He's waiting, on the far side, cracking knuckles
in rhythm with the crowd, and the referee keeps steering the fight toward him.

"That one's mine," Merdou and Ashren say at the same time, and then look at each other.

---

**⟨ CHOICE 5 — The gauntlet's worst is waiting. Who takes him? ⟩**

**1 — "MINE. Wash your hands when it's your turn."**
*Merdou eats the worst of it — and whatever it costs him, he carries into the champion's fight.*

**2 — "Yours, bird. Show them what patience looks like."**
*Ashren takes him — handicapped, no flame, a hooked net in play, and forty rows watching.*

**3 — "Screw the rules. TOGETHER."**
*Break sanction, fight as a pair — and turn the championship into something no referee owns.*

«ON GAUNTLET DEFEAT — the loss branch»
The sand comes up. The bell doesn't ring for you.
You wake below the arena, in the cells that don't appear on any tour, iron on your wrist and a
brand-tag on the iron: a debt number. There are other men down here. Quiet men. One of them
nods at the tag. "Dawn boat," he says, like the weather.
The champion's fight still comes — Gups prefers his enemies pre-ruined and the crowd was
promised a show — but it comes with the number on your record, from a cell, on his schedule.
Some things stay: the number stays. What the island saw stays.
