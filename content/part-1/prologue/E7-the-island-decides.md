# E7 — THE ISLAND DECIDES

**THE COLOSSEUM. AFTERWARD.**

«IF C6 = 1» It ends with a man. Just a man — shirt gone, arm hanging wrong, ribs made of bad
news — walking through the last of the lightning because someone had to, catching the falling
barrier on his back, and then walking THROUGH the panicking champion's guard with the one thing
he saved all week.
"CLOSING TIME!!"
The haymaker lands like a dropped mast. Gups goes down through his own mosaic, and the circuit
dies with a sound like a kettle coming off the boil.

«IF C6 = 2» It ends with horns.
They rise off Merdou's brow like something surfacing — black, curved, WRONG for a human head and
exactly right for the belt he's worn for seven years — and the man beneath them catches the
Bell's arc on them, walks it down through his boots into the sand, and puts his fist through the
champion's guard.
"CLOSING TIME!!"
Gups goes down. Forty rows have gone completely silent, every face asking the same question, and
the question has horns.

«IF C6 = 3» It ends with fire.
Ashren opens his wings in front of forty rows and BURNS — a wall of white-orange flame that
swallows the Bell's arc whole, a shape out of dead history hanging over the sand — and behind
that light, Merdou crosses the arena and finishes it.
"CLOSING TIME!!"
When the flame drops, the island is looking at a Lunarian. Alive. Real. Standing on their sand
next to a grinning bald man, saying nothing, because there is nothing left to hide.

The dust settles. The referee crawls for the exit. Somewhere under the champion's gate, the men
in the cells are pressed against the bars, watching the manifest post burn where the arc caught
it.

And the island — forty rows of dyers and fishermen and grannies and collectors gone very pale —
looks down at the two men standing over eleven years of Unbeaten, and waits to be told what
happens now.

"Well," Ashren says. He is bleeding, smoking «IF C6=3: literally», and his voice still hasn't
come back from the cells. "You wanted to do something big."

«IF C7 = 1 — LIBERATION»
The bonfire on the arena steps burns all afternoon. Priya's ledgers go in armfuls, and each
armful gets its own cheer. Joro's page — «IF joro ran: fetched from the collectors' office;
IF saved: brought by Joro himself, grinning; IF public cause: carried at the front like a flag» —
goes in last. Below the gate the cells stand open. Sergeant Hale stands at the fire's edge with
eleven years of sincerely believed reports coming apart in his head, and doesn't stop anyone.
Gups: the island votes to put him on his own dawn boat — or worse — and the two of them decide
his ticket. The grannies reclaim the arena by evening. There is talk of holding the Bloom Haul in
it, the way the Legion meant.

«IF C7 = 2 — NEW MANAGEMENT»
It takes one afternoon to learn how smoothly the machine runs when its operator is afraid of you.
Priya presents the ledgers with a bookkeeper's bow, one professional to another. The Toll
collects. The Ferry contract sits on the table, unsigned but undead, an asset now. When they walk
into the Copper Kettle that evening, the room goes one notch quieter, and Auntie Marn pours
without being asked and without meeting anyone's eyes. Merdou notices. Merdou says nothing.
Gups keeps his life as an employee of his own arena — walking proof of the new order.

«IF C7 = 3 — TOPPLE & WALK»
They break the machine in the most literal way available: Merdou tears the betting windows out of
the wall with DEAD LIFT while Ashren reads the crowd the ledger's greatest hits, loudly,
theatrically, with commentary. Then they leave it — all of it — the arena to the grannies, the
Toll to nobody, the cells open, Gups deposed and alive and suddenly very small on an island with
no rules about him anymore and long memories. The island watches them start down the Six Hundred
Steps and doesn't know whether to cheer or panic. Some of both, in the end.
