# STING — THE COIN LANDS

**THE NORTH BLUE. OPEN WATER. THREE DAYS LATER.**

*In the Great Pirate Era, news travels on wings.*

A News Coo banks out of a gray sky and drops a rolled paper onto the deck of a ship — a good
ship, well-kept, sails patched neat as Sunday clothes.

A lean hand picks it up.

«IF mark = LIBERATED» **PIT ISLAND FREED — CHAMPION FALLS, DEBTS BURNED.**
«IF mark = OWNED» **COIN TOSS UNDER NEW MANAGEMENT — WHO ARE THEY?**
«IF mark = TOPPLED» **THE CROWN STANDS EMPTY — CHAMPION DEPOSED, ISLAND MASTERLESS.**

«IF exposure.merdou» Beneath the headline, a smudged witness sketch: a bald giant. With horns.
«IF exposure.ashren» Beneath the headline, one word repeated down the column like a struck
match: *LUNARIAN?*
«IF exposure = whisper» Beneath the headline, a line of gossip: *witnesses speak of sparks...*
«IF secrets intact» Beneath the headline, two names nobody's ever printed before, and no
pictures. Yet.

The lean man reads it once. Reads it again.

Then he starts to laugh — "Kohohoho! KOHOHOHO!!" — a laugh like a cough that made something of
itself, getting louder, until someone off-deck yells:

"Captain? What is it?"

He holds up the paper to people we cannot see, grinning like a boy on harvest morning.
