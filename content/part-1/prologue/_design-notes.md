# PROLOGUE DESIGN NOTES (private — never player-facing)

Storyboard passes per scene (per SCENE_GRAMMAR rule 3). Compact: location / objective /
interruption / prop / reactions / comedy / danger / withheld / change / final image.

**E1** Pit at night. Obj: Merdou wants the dye-girl's attention (not the win). Interrupt:
Brakko's steel. Props: whiskey glass (spilled = the turn), chain fists. Reactions: announcer →
grandmother stops counting → bookies stop shouting (order matters). Comedy: losing while
flirting; "PACING myself." Danger: steel in fists-only. Withheld: why Gups monitors his sobriety
(pays E4); the belt's meaning. Change: Gups cancels again = the ducking pattern on-screen.
Final image: Merdou setting his feet, laughing.

**E2** Kettle. Obj: dinner (Merdou), the barmaid (Ashren). Interrupt: collectors. Props: stove/
marrow, cat, silver cup of beer, the papers on Joro's table. Reactions: accordion misses a note
→ Marn's pour stops → nobody says the boat's name. Comedy: cat-on-head flirting, flame sputter.
Danger: quiet coats, the unnamed Ferry. Withheld: where the Ferry goes. Change: Joro's fate
chosen; the rot is now visible. Final image: still spoon, watched.

**E3** Steps at night. Obj: get home drunk. Interrupt: the argument turns real. Props: the dog
with the glove, the bottle, the coat coming off. Reactions: the dog (comic deflation at peak —
sneeze, glove down). Comedy: front half only; dies exactly at the needle. Danger: emotional
only — first scene with zero physical threat (variety). Withheld: everything but the patricide
fact and the wings (disclosure ledger). Change: the pact; a sworn promise. Final image: bottle
held out.

**E4** Crown gate, morning. Obj: force the match. Interrupt: Hale's defense; the crowd's
gout-chorus. Props: portraits, silk cloth, the gate itself. Reactions: baskets set down → kid
runs grinning → crowd recites excuses. Comedy: hungover shouting at a building; the gout.
Danger: Gups's auditor-stare drop; champion's-rules trap accepted. Withheld: what the weigh-in
really is (pays E5). Change: the match exists, terms set, week routed. Final image: Gups's slow
gray turn; Merdou looking for a hand-pump.

**E5** Colosseum, fight day. Obj: survive the gauntlet clean. Interrupt: the referee's steering;
hooked net. Props: the FILTHY ceremonial cup (hygiene trait beats the drug — comedy from
character), flail, net. Reactions: crowd's fix-expectation turning into fight-noise. Comedy:
the cup horror. Danger: real (loss branch lives here — cells + brand). Withheld: the floor's
secret (pays E6). Change: gauntlet resolved, worst-matchup cost assigned. Final image: the big
one cracking knuckles / or the cell ceiling.

**E6** Colosseum, main event. Obj: beat eleven years of lie. Interrupts: the floor bites; the
cells become visible; the Bell. Props: mosaic circuit, stone rows, bell+chain, manifest post.
Reactions: crowd laughs AT the champion (first time) → ears back → crowd sees him clearly in
phase 3. Comedy: the stone-row dance, MANNERS!!. Danger: maximal, and aimed at bystanders by
design (the coward's true shape). Withheld: none left about Gups — the fight IS the reveal.
Change: champion falls; a half-second choice decides whose secret pays for the crowd. Final
image: the arc leaping bell→tusks→stands.

**E7** Same sand, after. Obj: answer the island. Interrupt: none — the one scene where the world
waits for THEM (deliberate stillness after E6). Props: ledgers/bonfire | Priya's bow | betting
windows torn out. Reactions: per fork (cheer / fear-quiet / cheerful argument). Comedy: fork 3's
demolition commentary. Danger: moral only. Withheld: what the mark will cost later. Change: the
World-Mark — the campaign's first permanent word. Final image: per fork (fire / flag / empty
Crown).

**E8** Jetty, dawn. Obj: leave. Interrupt: the boat with five dead. Props: pipe, mending-needle,
carved gull, Marn's paintbrush (C8-3). Reactions: hats off, slow argument, the island watching
them decide. Comedy: cats boarding like ticket-holders (gentle; scene stays quiet). Danger:
none — grief scene (variety). Withheld: nothing; the sea just does that (Mert lock). Change:
boat acquired three different ways; departure. Final image: island going small, laugh over water.

**E9** Sting. One narrator line, one paper, one laugh, out.

## Register guardrails applied throughout
Peaks get the shortest lines (E3's four words). No interiority anywhere. Narrator: E1 card, E4/E5
cards, E9 one line — ≈2% of words. Villain gets the paragraphs (Gups E4/E6). Comedy inside the
climax (stone-row dance) per Oda's own stated method. Damage as inventory, not poetry. No
manufactured quotables; no "not X but Y"; no personified abstractions; island texture from
work/family/weather roots (Sunblind, step-shanties, maybe-buns) not gambling-only.
