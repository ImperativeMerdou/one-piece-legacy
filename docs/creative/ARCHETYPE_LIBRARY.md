# ARCHETYPE AND VIBE LIBRARY

**Status:** GOVERNED — operational (Phase 3A, 2026-07-20, Fable). Abstract role-components for
assembling original characters. **Archetypes are ingredients, never characters.** Every
character combines ≥3 components across different columns; reject any character whom ONE canon
comparison explains more than half of (the "50% rule," per Mert's Phase 3 order).

## 1. Component columns

**Social role:** civic hero · administrator · keeper/feeder · liaison · debtor · enforcer ·
bookkeeper · elder authority · apprentice/kid · outsider passing as local · clergy/custom-keeper.

**Comic role:** oblivious bulldozer · deflating pedant · competent person with one absurd
blind spot · dignity-loser (pratfall magnet) · straight man drowning in chaos · reluctant
pet-magnet · euphemism machine · repeater (escalating catchphrase) · wrong-audience performer.

**Emotional role:** witness who stayed · believer in the lie · lapsed believer · the one who
profits and knows it · grief-carrier who won't name it · protector running out of strength ·
child who sees clearly · latecomer to their own feelings.

**Combat role:** wall · timer (dangerous after N turns) · geography-user · counter-puncher ·
swarm-handler · protected asset (never fights, must be reached) · off-switch (one trick that
ends fights) · attrition monster.

**Public reputation vs. private contradiction (pick a PAIR):** beloved ↔ predatory · feared ↔
gentle · joke ↔ lethal · loyal ↔ bought · humble ↔ vain · corrupt-presumed ↔ sincere ·
protector-presumed ↔ absent.

## 2. Assembly rules

1. Pick components from ≥3 columns; the CONTRADICTION pair is mandatory for recurring cast.
2. Add one **ordinary-behavior loop** (a job routine seen at least twice) and one **prop**.
3. Voice fingerprint before first line (see `reference/distilled/DIALOGUE_FINGERPRINT.md`).
4. Wounds/tics/tragic history are NOT slots to fill — add one only if the story spends it
   (RESEARCH_INDEX warning against the mandatory-wound recipe stands).
5. Run the 50% rule: name the nearest canon character; list what the comparison does NOT
   explain; if the unexplained remainder is less than half the character, redesign.
