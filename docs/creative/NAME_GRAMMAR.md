# NAME GRAMMAR

**Status:** GOVERNED — operational (Phase 3A, 2026-07-20, Fable under delegated authority).
How this project names people, places, attacks, and factions so they read as One Piece-world
names without copying any canon name. Canon names cited below are ANALYSIS ANCHORS only —
never reusable.

## 1. Canon naming patterns (abstracted, with anchors)

1. **Real-world roots worn loudly.** Historical pirates (Teach, Newgate, Rocks), real
   surnames bent one notch (Zoro ← François l'Olonnais via "Zorro" energy; Bege ← Capone).
   → Rule: original names may borrow REAL nautical/historical/professional roots, then bend
   spelling or pairing until no single source is identifiable.
2. **Food, object, animal wordplay.** Sanji's kitchen numbers, the Charlotte dessert dynasty,
   Crocodile/Gecko Moria/Caribou — the word's SOUND matters more than the pun's cleverness.
   → Rule: one wordplay layer max; the name must survive people not getting the joke.
3. **Phonetic rhythm.** Two to four syllables dominate; doubled vowels and open endings
   (Luffy, Nami, Ace) for sympathetic figures; consonant clusters and plosives (Crocodile,
   Kaido, Gups) for threats. Comic henchmen get bouncing double consonants (Buggy, Brakko).
4. **Shoutability.** Every important name is tested by being SCREAMED across a battlefield
   and whispered in a death scene. Names that fail either register go to extras.
5. **Family-name order flexes by culture** (Monkey D. Luffy vs. Roronoa Zoro vs. Charlotte
   Linlin) — a family-first name signals dynasty weight (our Archon D. Merdou follows this).
6. **Epithet construction:** [quality/object] + [role/animal/legend] — "Fire Fist," "Pirate
   Hunter," "Surgeon of Death." Epithets are PRESS INVENTIONS in-world: they may misreport,
   flatten, or flatter, and characters react to their epithets (deny, embrace, resent).
7. **Titles as satire:** institutional titles run pompous (Colosseum General, Champion of the
   Old Legion's arena) and the street shortens them disrespectfully (Baldy).
8. **Place names are job descriptions:** Water 7, Sabaody GROVE numbers, Loguetown ("prologue
   and epilogue") — places named for what they do or what happened, in blunt compound nouns.
   Our island practice matches: Gullwharf, Petal Row, the Six Hundred Steps.
9. **Absurdity is load-bearing:** a serious arc can hang on a silly name (Gups) because the
   world treats the name with total sincerity. Never wink at a name in narration.
10. **Laugh = signature:** distinct laugh syllables function as name tags (canon practice,
    per SBS-era statements). Ours: UHUHUHU / Kihihihi / Mmohohoho / Kohohoho / Ohohoho —
    each unique per character, never shared.

## 2. Rejection list (auto-fail, no discussion)

Generic AI-fantasy defaults: **Kael, Silas, Elara, Draven, Thorne, Seraphina, Lyra, Kira,
Aria(n), Corvus, Ravena**, any apostrophe-name (K'thar), any -iel/-wyn/-eth elf suffix.
Generic faction paint: **Crimson/Shadow/Iron/Silver/Obsidian/Ebon [Hand/Blade/Order/Guard]**.
Direct canon lifts or one-letter dodges (Zolo, Luffee, Gecko-anything). Renamed canon
characters (a "new" character whose kit+role+design decodes to one canon person).

## 3. NAME SCORECARD (0–50; per Mert's Phase 3 order)

| Criterion | Max |
|---|---|
| Memorable aloud (shout + whisper test) | 10 |
| Fits speaker and culture (island/faction/family register) | 10 |
| Visual or thematic hook (evokes a drawable thing or trait) | 10 |
| Comic potential (survives being yelled by Merdou) | 5 |
| Serious-scene survivability (survives a funeral) | 5 |
| Originality (no single traceable source) | 5 |
| Distance from existing canon names (sound + meaning) | 5 |

**Pass: ≥ 40 for major names; ≥ 32 for minor recurring names.** Generate ≥3 candidates
internally per important name; record the winner and one line on why in the relevant
brief/sidecar. Never ask Mert to approve ordinary names (his order); flag only names that
would enter LOCKED canon facts.

## 5. Attack-name grammar (per fighter, already in force)

Attack names follow the OWNER's register, not a house style: Merdou = tavern/work/blunt
imperatives (MANNERS!!, CLOSING TIME!!); Ashren = clerical/precise nouns (METRONOME,
OVERDUE); Gups = legal-civic sentence-passing (IVORY LAW, JUDGMENT BELL). New fighters get
a register statement BEFORE any attack is named. Finishers are named once per fight.
