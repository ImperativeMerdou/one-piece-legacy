## Fruit name (reduplicated naming convention)

**PROPOSED:** *Ushi Ushi no Mi, Mythical Zoan — Model: Bull of Heaven* — following canon's exact
convention for mythical models (Tori Tori: Phoenix; Hito Hito: Nika; Inu Inu: Okuchi-no-Makami; the
Ushi Ushi line already exists via Bison and Giraffe). The model name may also carry its Sumerian root,
**Gugalanna**, in formal/lore contexts. *(Final name is Mert's to lock.)*

## Type

**Mythical Zoan** *(authored — Mert chose Proposal B, 2026-07-13).*

## Core premise (singular)

He becomes the **Bull of Heaven** — the divine bull of the Epic of Gilgamesh, **the oldest recorded
myth on Earth** (a deliberate resonance for a game titled *Legacy*). The myth's blessing is dominion
over **the momentum of his own mass**: the **Unstoppable Charge** and the **Immovable Stand.**
*(Scope authored via Proposal B: the blessing governs what flows through his own body — he is not a
battlefield-wide momentum broker. Haki remains the primary weapon.)*

## Heirloom law (authored by Mert, 2026-07-13)

- The Bull of Heaven is **passed down every second generation as the protector of the family.**
- The dynasty holds **two Mythical Zoans in total**: the **Bull of Heaven** and the **Griffin** (now in
  Aslan). Both were **gifted to the family by an unknown, ultra-powerful person from the Void Century.**
- **The giver's identity is a SEALED MYSTERY** and must stay within the contract's Void-Century
  sprinkle-only cap: hints and dread, never the vault.
- **When eaten (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** the night of the patricide, seven years ago. The father —
  keeper of both heirlooms, who deemed Merdou unfit and used him for the dirty work — refused him;
  Merdou **stole both fruits**, killed him in the battle, **ate the Bull that same night** at age 20,
  and gave the Griffin to 11-year-old Aslan while fleeing.
- **Chain and recovery mechanism (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** **the same as the Elbaf Nidhöggr
  fruit** in canon. *(Claude verification flag: recent-canon reference — verify against the Elbaf
  chapters before this lore goes on-page; logged in the fact-check ledger.)*
- **Suppression (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** in the seven years since eating it, Merdou **barely
  used the fruit — 99% of his time spent in base form** (a horned titan in a pit fight would have
  unmasked him). His mastery at game start is shallow; the Bull's depth is **playable progression, not
  backstory** — and this is why a seven-year Mythical wielder is honestly non-OP at the starting line.
- **The previous holder** (the cycle before the skip): **SEALED MYSTERY** (Mert, 2026-07-13) — to be
  revealed by Mert later, if ever.
- **The central irony (noted for design):** the fruit exists to *protect the family* — and its current
  bearer is the man who shattered it.

## Applications (infinite from the single premise)

- **Forms:** man → **hybrid horned titan** (the Kaido-grade presence Mert wanted; the demon-horned belt
  finally answered) → full Bull of Heaven.
- **The Unstoppable Charge:** once committed, his mass cannot be arrested by anything it strikes —
  gates, walls, formations, larger bodies.
- **The Immovable Stand:** braced, he cannot be moved — a harbor's last wall; an army breaks against
  one man.
- **Momentum through the body:** force that strikes him while braced can be carried into the next
  charge or the next mace swing (the fight's economy: what you hit him with, he returns with horns on).
- The blunt arts: mace work from horseback-of-himself; carrying a collapsing mast; plowing a landslide
  off a village.

## Embarrassment mode (a funny / humiliating use)

**PROPOSED:** the commitment problem *is* the comedy — a charge, once committed, does not stop because
its owner changed his mind. Merdou has, on record, charged through the correct wall into the incorrect
building, and once off a dock (a Devil Fruit user, off a dock). Also: the horns emerge on their own
when his Archon temper spikes — *ikari* rising — which makes poker games and diplomacy difficult.

## Oppression mode (a terrifying use)

**PROPOSED:** inevitability. No burst, no roar — the Bull simply starts **walking**, and everyone
watching understands that nothing they own, love, or hide behind will change the direction of that
walk. On the evil route, this is the fruit's whole horror: a man who cannot be stopped choosing not to
stop.

## Weaknesses — one systemic, one situational

**PROPOSED:**
1. **Systemic — the Commitment Law:** the blessing obeys the myth. The Bull of Heaven, once sent,
   could not be recalled. A committed charge cannot stop, slow, or turn — even by Merdou's own will —
   until it lands or expends itself. The Immovable Stand roots him: while he cannot be moved, he also
   cannot dodge, step, or retreat. Each mode locks the other out. Every use is a decision he must
   finish.
2. **Situational — the Bull is earthbound:** the blessing needs ground under it. Deprive him of footing
   — open water, air, collapsing terrain, ice too thin for his mass — and the myth goes mute; he is
   just a big man with Haki (standard sea/Seastone weaknesses apply on top).

## Awakening (thematic cost, if any)

**PROPOSED:** awakened, the blessing extends beyond his body — the Charge becomes a **stampede the
world joins** (what he passes is carried with him: rubble, ships, the army behind him), and the Stand
anchors **the terrain itself** (the cliff does not crumble; the tide waits). The thematic cost follows
the myth: in Gilgamesh, unleashing the Bull of Heaven brought calamity down on those who dared use it —
awakening is loud enough that **the world's apex hears it**, and every awakened use spends his body
brutally. The OP flip arrives late and bills its rider. *(Awakening stages and their arc placement are
Mert's to author.)*

## Whose identity this externalizes

Merdou's, precisely: the **stubbornness of all Archons** made divine law (the man who will not stop; the
man who will not move); the horns he has worn on his belt for years; the family's *protector* mantle
carried by the family's destroyer; the oldest legacy in human storytelling strapped to a man whose game
is called *Legacy*. And the pun survives the fruit-change: his temper is still *ikari* — the horns rise
with the wrath.

## Strip test (remove the fruit — is it still the same person?)

**Passes.** Strip the Bull and Merdou remains a pit-fighting wall of a man with dormant Conqueror's, a
mace, a dead father, and a dream of the throne. The fruit amplifies his identity; it does not supply
it. *(This was the design requirement — Haki-first — and the scoped blessing preserves it.)*
