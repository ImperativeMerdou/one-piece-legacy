## OLD CESTO — the sincere believer (new)

**Assembly:** keeper/feeder + elder · dignity-keeper with one absurd blind spot (the
propaganda) · believer in the lie whose faith cracks · non-combatant (retired wall) ·
beloved ↔ complicit-by-gratitude. Nearest canon influence: none dominant (the
grateful-client mechanism is Gups-brief §rewards made flesh). 50% rule: PASS.
**Design:** ~60, retired pit fighter — "Cesto the Basket" for how he caught punches; wrecked
knuckles, flour to the elbows; runs the Highbasket maybe-bun bakery. Eleven years ago a
career-ending loss put him in the ledgers; **Gups bought the debt out quietly** and got him
the oven lease [the reward machine — Gups brief]. Portrait of the General framed over the
ovens, dusted daily. Sincerely, warmly certain Gups saved his life — because he did, and
that fact is the trap. **Want:** to have been worth saving. **Flaw:** gratitude that
outvotes his eyes. **Comedy:** the civic-suspicion office of baker (one bun in six is
bitter; he takes accusations personally); narrates dough like a cornerman ("don't crowd it,
let it work"). **Serious mechanism:** faith shown cracking through OBJECTS, never speeches —
the portrait hangs crooked after the storm, face-down in fight week, and his E3S8 vote (per
fork) is one sentence he has to sit down to say. **Voice:** cornerman cadence — short
encouragements to inanimate things; calls everyone "champ" except Gups ("the General").
**Scorecard:** silhouette 12/15 · voice 12/15 · ordinary 9/10 · comedy 8/10 · serious 9/10 ·
want 9/10 · flaw 9/10 · culture 5/5 · collision 4/5 · function 4/5 · originality 5/5 =
**87 PASS** (floors met). Name: Cesto = basket (Old Legion root; Highbasket) — **43/50.**

## PINCH — the kid (new)

**Assembly:** apprentice/kid · repeater-with-escalation (does everyone's commentary) · child
who sees clearly · non-combatant · joke ↔ the island's memory. 50% rule: PASS (no single
canon kid explains him).
**Design:** 11, **Joro's little brother** — the wharf's loudest fence-slat kid (he boos
Brakko in E1S1 before he has a name). Nicknamed for pinching bait as a toddler; runs
deliveries for Cesto up the Steps without stopping [step-shanty kid verses]. Does dead-on
imitations: the announcer, Hale's cough, Merdou's UHUHUHU (which enrages/delights Merdou).
Plays Ferryman tag until E2S4, after which he won't, and nobody asks why [the island's dread
made visible in a child's game dying]. **Want:** to matter at the wharf like the grown-ups.
**Function:** makes Joro's debt a family stake; the belief that never cools when the island
does (E2S7's ember); collector-watch captain in fight week.
**Scorecard:** silhouette 11/15 · voice 12/15 · ordinary 9/10 · comedy 9/10 · serious 8/10 ·
want 8/10 · flaw 6/10 (recklessness) · culture 5/5 · collision 4/5 · function 3/5 ·
originality 5/5 = **84 PASS.** Name: Pinch (wharf-nickname grammar) — **44/50.**

## TAM — the almost-sailor (new)

**Assembly:** apprentice · dignity-loser (seasick on a jetty once, never lived down) ·
latecomer to his own feelings · non-combatant · joke ↔ the future the island risks.
**Design:** 16, Herrel's sister's boy, signed for his FIRST winter run. Carves birds badly
and constantly [the carved gull under the bow is his — v1 prop given provenance]. Asks
fighters sincere questions at wrong moments (E1S3: "is fighting scarier than the sea?").

saga's living grief — and pointedly NOT recruited (five seats, two men [L]; the sea is not
done teaching him).

## HERREL'S CREW — the doomed sailors (expanded from v1's off-page five)

**Herrel** — skipper, Marn's cousin's husband's line; pipe permanently unlit at the wheel
(lights it only in harbor — "the sea gets no smoke"); does debt arithmetic out loud in the
Kettle because hiding numbers is city behavior. Voice: forecast cadence — everything, love
included, delivered as weather. Scorecard **84 PASS** (minor-major boundary).
**Bruna** — mate; arm-wrestles for gear repairs and is banned from three tables; louder
than Merdou once a night, which he respects. **Cope** — old hand; mends nets through every
conversation, punctuates gossip with hook-pulls. **Mott** — quiet; feeds Ashren's cats his
own catch-scraps [the animals grieve too, E3S2, wordless].
Bruna/Cope/Mott are deliberately silhouette+voice+habit designs (no trauma slots invented —
`SCORECARDS.md §3` rule); their deaths are the sea's plain cruelty [L], their history is two
episodes of songs, scraps, and arm-wrestling. Names: Herrel [existing W] · Bruna 41 ·
Cope 42 · Mott 41 — all pass minor threshold.

## PRIYA — delta (pleasant-profiteer expansion; base in island/Gups briefs)

Add: at NOON she arbitrates the grannies' coin-flip disputes for free and is scrupulously,
famously fair — the island's most trusted neutral. At FOUR she fixes odds and runs the Toll
ledgers. Both facts are true; she sees no contradiction ("Fair flips are good for business.
So are unfair odds. Different products."). Private pleasure: she is assembling, flip by
recorded flip, the island's only actuarial table of LUCK, for no client but herself.
Function: C6's information gate; E3S8's professional bow / absence / enmity per route.

## GUPS — humanization deltas (per Phase 3 order; base brief stands)

Unrelated private pleasure: **pressed flowers** — a Maybe Flower folio, one bloom per year
on the island, annotated sweet-or-bitter; he flips a coin for which to believe.
Person he genuinely values: **Cesto** — the one debtor he saved for no strategic reason and
has never collected on; he takes tea at the bakery unannounced twice a year and is, for
forty minutes, almost the man the posters claim. Petty hatred: **whistling on the Steps**
(it calls the wind; he has fined it). Routine: dawn ledger reconciliation before any staff
arrive — the only work he trusts no one with. Irrational belief: counts stairs in threes;
a flight ending off-count means postpone (three of his six cancellations were REAL — the
superstition, not the cowardice, which makes the cowardice harder to prove). Refuses to
sacrifice until panic: the LEDGERS (brief: the only fights he never dodged). Selfish
improvement that genuinely helped: the **Crown cisterns** — built so his arena never
lacks water; plumbed half of Highbasket's terraces on the way; the flower trade's yield
doubled. None of this redeems him; all of it makes him eleven years deep in the island.
