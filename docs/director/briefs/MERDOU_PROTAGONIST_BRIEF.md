## Origin

- The **Archon D. Dynasty**: an ancient house tracing back to **the rebellion — one of the first houses
  in the war against the World Government's founding.** The house **split**: one branch **sold out**
  (elevated into the Celestial Dragons), one branch **rebelled** (and stayed rebels).
- Since that era, the rebel branch has lived as **restaurant owners, tavern keepers, and warrior poets**
  — masters of cuisine, legendary drinkers, funny, explosive, impulsive, quick to anger, deep-down good,
  fiery — and **all of them stubborn.**
- **Mother:** a **fallen, broke, ruined Celestial Dragon** — now working as a **maid, cleaning a rival
  house's home in Mary Geoise.** **She is owned** (confirmed, Mert 2026-07-13): the decades of Celestial
  bribery ended in the family's fall and her leash — her residence in the Holy Land *is* the servitude.
- **Who knows the family's truth (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** only the world's apex — the **Gorosei**;
  **Garp** and **Sengoku**; **Rayleigh** and other icons of the old era; **Kaido** and **Big Mom**;
  **Shanks — who knows the most** — and **Shamrock**. **The public does not know what the epithets
  mean:** the papers print "Fallen Prince" without knowing which throne. *(Every named knower is a canon
  character — any on-screen use still requires its own locked `CANON_ENCOUNTER_BRIEF.md`.)*
- **Father:** dead — **killed by Merdou seven years ago.** Keeper of **both** family heirlooms: the
  **Bull of Heaven** and the **Griffin** (rebellion-era treasures). Wielded **advanced Conqueror's
  Haki** — **deliberately hidden** (authored, Mert 2026-07-13): a **promise the mother and father made
  to each other** to lay low on purpose, while the family was **paid off, bribed, and used by the
  Celestials for decades.** **He did not see Merdou fit to be anything** — used him for all the dirty
  work and overworked him. They clashed; deep down they loved each other.
- **The night (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** Merdou — refused on the rebellion and deemed unfit —
  **stole both fruits**, fought his father (**the huge battle**; the advanced-Conqueror's slash across
  his chest), **killed him, ate the Bull of Heaven that same night**, and pressed the Griffin into
  11-year-old **Aslan's** hands while fleeing. He was 20. **Still SEALED:** what the twins believe
  happened, and whatever deeper circumstances Mert has not yet revealed.
- **Siblings** (not seen in seven years):
  - **Aslan**, 18 — theatric, majestic, prince-like; blond, blue-eyed, beautiful. A young pirate
    **captaining his own crew** on the same Supernova trajectory (independent — see contract amendment,
    14 at Sabaody). Ate the **Griffin fruit the night the father died**, at age 11. Dormant Conqueror's
    Haki. Open-minded and hugely ambitious.
  - **Melissa and Metehan**, 23, twins — **Marines.** Both **Logia users, medium-tier strong.** They
    **hate and despise Merdou** for the father's death. What exactly they believe happened:
    **SEALED MYSTERY.**

## Public dream

**Power** *(authored by Mert, 2026-07-13 — Legacy Dial 1 is unblocked)*. The ambition to become **the
strongest in the world** — on the road to becoming an even bigger **tyrant or king than Kaido**, or
equivalent. The **tyrant-or-king fork lives inside the dream itself**: the same hunger can be steered
by the player toward a crown, a reign of terror, or something canon never saw.

## Private goal (and the gap between public and private)

**Partially SEALED.** At 20 he wanted to **restart the rebellion in this age**; at 27 he professes only
power and hunger. Whether the rebellion still smolders beneath the power-dream — and what the true gap
between the stated dream and the buried one is — remains Mert's to reveal.

## Wound (and how it stays portable in ordinary moments)

- **The patricide.** He killed his father; deep down they loved each other; he has "made peace-*ish*" —
  the *-ish* is the wound.
- **The contempt (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** his father never saw him fit to be anything — the
  dirty-work son, overworked and unchosen. The man now aiming at *strongest in the world* was first the
  boy deemed unworthy of the family he ended up carrying. *(Raw material for the blank pride/insecurity
  field — Mert's to shape.)*
- **Portable marker (authored):** a **huge slash scar across his hairy chest** — from his father's
  **advanced Conqueror's Haki slash.** He carries the last argument on his body, visible every time the
  crimson shirt hangs open.
- **Seven lost years:** hiding, drinking, drugs, women, odd jobs, pit fights, criminal friends — and
  seven years of silence toward every sibling.
- **Current state (authored, Mert 2026-07-13):** *bored of life; doesn't give a fuck anymore.*
  **Approved reading (Mert, 2026-07-13):** his "made peace-ish" and his boredom are the same thing —
  the peace of numbness, not of healing. The Kaido line is drawn: *Kaido is a man looking for a worthy
  death; Merdou is a man too numb to look for anything — until the sea makes him give a fuck again, or
  crowns him before it can.*
