# PROTAGONIST BRIEF — ARCHON D. MERDOU

> Rebuilt from the 2026-07-13 locked brief + Mert's Phase 2A interview rulings + Phase 2B
> delegation. Prior brief preserved in git history. SEALED mysteries remain sealed.

## Identity

- [L] **Archon D. Merdou**, 27. Fixed protagonist. "Archon D." dynasty name deliberately restored.
- [L] Epithet (planned first): **"The Fallen Prince"** — arrives with his first bounty, post-prologue;
  the public will not know which throne. He begins **locally known, globally nobody.**
- [W] Pit ring-name gag: he bills himself **"The Magnificent Merdou"**; the whole island just calls
  him **"Baldy"**, which enrages him every single time.

## Voice (the fingerprint — Mert-authored sample on record, Phase 2A)

- [L] Loud, crude, unfiltered. Short shouted declaratives. Zero filter: what he thinks, he says,
  on the spot. Talker, not grunter. R-rated tavern register.
- [L] Signature laugh: deep, booming — [W] canonical spelling **"UHUHUHU!!"** (test-in-scenes;
  Mert gave two spellings; this one reads best on the page; overturnable).
- [L] Reference sample (Mert, verbatim register): bored whiskey-chugging, "This sucks, no
  entertainment in this damn shithole," loud propositions to Ashren about fights vs. women,
  booming laugh, brawls, failed wooing.
- [L] Catchphrase: none forced. If one emerges naturally in scenes it may be promoted later.

## Present-tense character (game start)

- [L] **His numbness is LOUD.** Bored = restless complaining, brawl-seeking, whore-chasing.
  Never the quiet cool badass. Warm with Ashren; numb-and-bored default elsewhere.
- [L] Believes he is the greatest, manliest man alive — and is **embarrassed by sincere
  admiration**: gets angry, changes the subject.
- [L] Laughs at the absurd, creative, weird. Loves roasting and shit-talk.
- [L] Irritated by pettiness, weakness-as-surrender, and **aimlessness — deliberately his own
  flaw** (he hates his seven wasted years; Ashren may needle this).
- [L] Philosophy: nobody is ever done for — there is always a way out. What he despises is
  staying in the gutter. He **despises aimless cruelty.**
- [L] Hygiene-obsessed brawler: hates mess and dirt, washes his hands constantly.
- [L] Reads books; loves history and world-knowledge (the brute who knows things).
- [L] Seeks strong enemies; admires them **out loud, mid-fight**.
- [L] Hates Marines/Navy as institution; hates **Celestial Dragons above all**; respects
  individuals sincerely serving justice.
- [L] **Idolizes Kaido** — "strongest Emperor"; relates to him. (Not a Kaido clone; the game's
  differentiation line stands: Kaido seeks a worthy death, Merdou is numb until the sea wakes him.)
- [L] Pit record: **a losing record** — drunk arrivals, no-shows (whoring), distractions,
  stupidity. The island knows him as the loudmouth waste of monstrous talent.
- [L] Wrong beliefs about himself: that numbness is permanent; that the fun, goofy, good
  **protector** self is gone for good; that the road ahead is shit.

## Anger

- [L] Genuine Archon fire = **louder chaos**: horns rise and grow, volume up, furniture breaks.
  Never scary-calm. [W] Standing gag: post-rage, he is genuinely bothered by the mess he made.

## Fixed codes (never-list at game start)

- [L] **Never deliberately strikes a woman.** [W] Expression (original, not Sanji's chivalry):
  machismo logic — *"no manliness in it, nothing to prove."* He announces the code loudly, tanks
  hits from women while complaining at volume, restrains/dodges instead of striking.
- [L] **Never deliberately harms a child.**
- [L] **Never uses ranged weapons.** Melee only — blunt weapon and fists. Calls ranged fighting
  "pussy shit"; mocks shooters and runners. (Exploitable: smart ranged enemies can bait him.)
- [W] Never abandons Ashren; never kills the surrendered without player-built cause. On the long
  evil route these two can erode only through flagged crux choices; the woman/child codes are
  permanent bedrock.

## Player control

- [L] The player steers **all four**: actions, values, methods, relationships — inside his
  plausible range. No out-of-character menu options.

## Skills, habits, weaknesses

- [L] Ordinary skills: **cooking** (Archon tavern blood — the marrow rule) and **reading/world
  knowledge**.
- [L] Social weakness: **hilariously fails at wooing women.**
- [W] Foolish habit: bets on himself with money he doesn't have — not for the gambling, out of
  pure self-belief ("I'm the safest bet on this island!" — the island disagrees, loudly).
- [L] Combat restriction: melee-only (above). [W] Combat flaw: taunt-baitable — mockery of his
  manliness or his record can pull him out of position.
