# SAGA MAP — "THE COIN LANDS" (three-episode Coin Toss Island mini-saga)

3 episodes · 23 scenes + 3 stings · target 18,000–24,000 player-facing words across branches ·
13 choice nodes, each with branch-specific immediate-consequence beats (four-layer loop per
`docs/runtime/CHOICE_CONSEQUENCE_SYSTEM.md`) · Coin Toss Island → Loguetown.

**Locked beats honored [L]:** losing-record Merdou on-screen from scene one · tavern ignition ·
Gups beaten by Merdou and Ashren · player-decided takeover (LIBERATE / OWN / TOPPLE) + Gups's
fate player-decided · five-dead boat arrives as mundane sea tragedy (cold, water, time — no
mystery) · full mutual honesty by departure · departure to Loguetown · News-Coo → Groomed
Pirates sting · concealment-crisis model node · exactly-three choices at ordinary nodes.

**Phase 3 structural corrections implemented:** Gups is NOT met in person in Episode 1
(reputation-first, per REFERENCE_MATRIX §6) · the patricide confession moves to Episode 2,
behind two failed conversations and a forcing event (slow-burn rules 2–3) · Ashren's wings
answer is NOT symmetrical and NOT same-scene (rule 4) — it is his own act, one episode later ·
Episode 2 ends at the worst position of the saga · the doomed sailors live on-screen for two
episodes before the boat comes in · no player-facing phase labels anywhere.

## Spine

Seven wasted years (an island quietly eating its debtors) → the pit answer that makes the
champion's machine look at them (Ep1) → the machine's social war: bought, squeezed, disgraced,
beaten lawfully; the confession; the worst morning (Ep2) → the storm; the grief that turns the
island; the pact; the unduckable match; the fall of the Unbeaten; the player-shaped island;
five seats, two men; the world notices (Ep3).
