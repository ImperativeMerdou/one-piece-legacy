# ARC MAP — THE PROLOGUE: "THE COIN LANDS" (Episode One)

8 scenes + sting · target ~7,000–8,500 words incl. variants · Coin Toss Island → Loguetown.
Design rules honored: choice axes rotate (no alignment triples, no filler apathy slot); every
option changes an event/resource/relationship/route; histories surface progressively (no
symmetrical confession dump); full mutual Merdou–Ashren honesty exists **by departure** [L].

## Spine

Suffering (seven wasted years; an island quietly eating its debtors) → ignition ("fuck it") →
defiance (the unduckable match) → the argument made public (the spectacle is a lie) →
player-shaped liberation (the fork) → departure (five seats, two men) → the world notices (sting).

## Scene list

**E1 — "BALDY THE MAGNIFICENT" (the Pit, night).**
Cold open mid-match: Merdou is LOSING to a nobody — drunk, heckling his own opponent, distracted
by a woman in the crowd [losing record on-screen, voice established]. Ashren at the rail, roasting.
Gups in his box cancels his exhibition again ("the old injury") the moment he confirms Merdou is
sober-ish tonight. Turn: Brakko's hidden steel (fists-only pit) the instant Merdou gets serious.
**C1 (axis: TACTIC/RESOURCE):**
 1. Win big and loud — comeback show. → crowd love, Gups threat-alert ↑, purse lost (bet was against him).
 2. Win fast and ugly. → goons afraid, Gups accelerates rigging plans, crowd unsettled.
 3. Throw the match — Ashren bet on the nobody. → fat purse, reputation stays trash, Gups relaxes; Ashren smug all evening.
State: `purse`, `rep.pit`, `gups.alert`, `bond.ashren` nuance. All three change E2's opening and E4's odds.

**E2 — "THE COPPER KETTLE" (Gullwharf tavern, same night).**
Branched opener per C1. Island alive: step-shanty night, Sunblind aftermath, maybe-buns, Auntie
Marn. Merdou commandeers the stove [L beat: cooking/marrow]. Ashren's flirt engine → flame-sputter
→ cat sabotage. The rot arrives quietly: two collectors at Joro's table — the Anvil was "a sure
thing," the nets and winter lines are due; the word **Ferry** is not said, and everyone hears it.
**C2 (axis: RESOURCE / PUBLIC STANCE / INFORMATION):**
 1. Cover Joro's marker quietly (needs `purse`; if broke, Auntie Marn fronts it — debt to her).
 2. Stand and name the fix, loudly, in front of everyone. → the challenge effectively begins tonight; `gups.alert`↑↑, crowd emboldened.
 3. Quietly tell Joro when the Ferry sails and how to miss it. → Joro runs; collectors humiliated later; retaliation lands on the Kettle in E4 unless handled.
State: `joro.fate`, `heat.collectors`, `bond.marn`, `worldmark.seed`. Each option = a different visible island in E4/E7.

**E3 — "FUCK IT" (the Six Hundred Steps, drunk, late).**
The climb home. Ashren needles the wasted years [L irony]; the argument turns real; Merdou says
the sentence — **"I killed my father."** — and no more (progressive disclosure; details stay
banked). Ashren answers with an act, not a speech: wings out over the cliff dark, and his dream
aloud [L]. The pact: "fuck it — we finally do something with our lives." [L]
**C3 (axis: PROMISE):** what Merdou swears to the dream:
 1. "I'll help you rebuild them. All of it." → Promise Ledger: `promise.tribe` (kept/broken tracked all game).
 2. "The dead don't come back. The ones still breathing need somewhere to stand — I'll get you that." → hard-truth respect; `bond.ashren` friction now, foundation later.
 3. "Help me take the throne and your people get an island — that's the deal." → `promise.island-for-tribe`: binds both dreams into one contract.
No apathy slot. Ends on the pact, not a speech.

**E4 — "THE UNBEATEN" (the Crown, next morning, hungover).**
Public callout in front of the arena portraits. Gups — cornered by the crowd's memory of years of
cancellations, and confident in his machine — ACCEPTS, on **champion's rules**: sanctioned match
in one week, his arena, his referee, his "medical weigh-in." Sgt. Hale beat: sincere Marine
propaganda in one exchange. Ducking history dramatized (the crowd recites his excuses along with
him).
**C4 (axis: ROUTE — how the week is spent; sets E5/E6 entry states):**
 1. Train sober under Ashren's plan. → `cond.sharp` (fight buffs); collectors run wild on the Steps unchecked that week (`island.pressure`↑, Joro-thread worsens if unresolved).
 2. Spend the week breaking the fix — shadow Priya, expose the ledger tricks. → `fix.exposed` (E5 gauntlet loses its dirtiest tools; crowd truth-bond ↑); enter the arena tired (`cond.worn`).
 3. Bet everything they own on themselves at Priya's window. → `allin=true`: a win ruins the house — Gups cannot afford ANY fix that's traceable; fight becomes honest but the stakes are their whole future (loss = the Ferry, for them).
**E4b (optional micro-node, Pure Continuation):** Kettle war-council flavor per route.

**E5 — "CHAMPION'S RULES" (arena undercard, fight day).**
The gauntlet: Brakko + picked goons; the drugged "ceremonial water" — which Merdou refuses
because the cup is FILTHY [hygiene trait pays off; the fix whiffs on comedy, not luck]. Ashren's
public handicap on display (no flame, "The Persuader" showcase).
**C5 (axis: RELATIONSHIP/SACRIFICE — who eats the worst matchup):**
 1. Merdou takes it. → `injury.merdou.ribs` carried into E6 (and to Loguetown).
 2. Ashren takes it. → flame-temptation beat; `ashren.strain`; if he slips even a spark: `exposure.whisper` seed.
 3. Break sanction — fight it together, openly. → forfeits "champion's rules"; E6 becomes an unsanctioned Crown brawl (route variant: no referee, no bell ritual — different Gups phase-1).
**LOSS BRANCH [meaningful defeat possible]:** losing the gauntlet = waking in the debt cells
under the arena with the Ferry candidates; `debt.brand` permanent state; E6 entered from the
cells, angrier and worse-positioned. Not game-over; never cost-free.

**E6 — "JUDGMENT BELL" (the Crown, main event).**
Three phases per Gups brief: Showman (scripted spectacle; floor tricks; "accidental"
interference) → Monster (first unscripted pain; IVORY LAW; genuinely a North Blue monster) →
Panic King (the myth cracks in public; THUNDER TOLL floods the mosaic; JUDGMENT BELL aimed
through the stands — the showman's restraint was the only mercy he had). Mechanic discovery:
the mosaic is circuitry. Mid-fight, the arena's underbelly is seen — the cells, the Ferry
manifest [Ashren's crusader switch trips: his rage register, first public glimpse of what's
underneath the cool].
**C6 (axis: SACRIFICE/INFORMATION — the concealment crisis [L model node]):** the Bell is coming
down on the stands:
 1. Merdou holds the disguise — pit-craft and bare will; win ugly. → `injury.merdou.arm` + `cond.broken` to Loguetown; secrets intact.
 2. The horns come out. → `exposure.merdou`: witnesses, "what IS he" island memory, News-Coo fuel; the demon-horned belt suddenly makes sense to everyone.
 3. Ashren burns — HIS choice, offered in one line ("Catch him. I'll light the sky."). Player accepts or refuses his sacrifice. → `exposure.ashren`: a lit Lunarian is a confessed Lunarian; hunted-flag planted for Part I.
Gups falls [L outcome] — differently per phase-3 route and C6 choice.

**E7 — "THE ISLAND DECIDES" (the Crown, morning after).**
The island kneels to its new champions — and waits. The fork + its first consequence in ONE
scene: choose, then watch the island answer before the scene ends.
**C7 (axis: ROUTE — the first World-Mark [L]):**
 1. **LIBERATE** — burn the ledgers on the arena steps; the Ferry contract dies; Priya flees; Hale's worldview cracks on-screen. → `mark.cointoss=LIBERATED`.
 2. **OWN** — the Toll has new management; Priya works for them now; the Kettle goes quiet when they enter. → `mark.cointoss=OWNED` (+ standing income state, + the island's fear).
 3. **TOPPLE & WALK** — break the machine, crown no one; give the arena back to the grannies and the Bloom Haul; leave them free and leaderless. → `mark.cointoss=TOPPLED`.
Gups's personal fate is chosen INSIDE each fork (sub-beat, 2-option each, per locked
player-decided rule — exile/servitude/handover shapes, fork-appropriate).

**E8 — "FIVE SEATS, TWO MEN" (Gullwharf, dawn).**
Departure prep shaped by the fork. The five-dead boat [L: mundane sea tragedy, no mystery]:
a five-man fishing boat drifts in on the dawn tide, crew dead of the sea's plain cruelty —
staged at the wharf among people who knew such deaths. The town wants the boat burned
(superstition); it's sound, and it's the only hull free men can take.
**C8 (axis: PUBLIC STANCE / INFORMATION):**
 1. Bury the five in Highbasket, names unknown, before sailing. → the island remembers; `bond.island` echo in later papers.
 2. Give them to the sea, pirate-fashion, and take the boat with the town watching. → first public act *as pirates*; rumor seed.
 3. Ask the wharf who they were first. → learns one was Auntie Marn's cousin's crew; the boat gains a name the town gives it; `boat.named` (the only route where the boat is christened by the island).
Departure: the island's new state visible from the water [per-fork closing image]. Merdou at the
rail, looking back, then forward — the wrong-beliefs beat played in one line of dialogue, not
narration.
