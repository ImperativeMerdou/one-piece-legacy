# RESEARCH INDEX — reliability guide to the archived corpus

**Status:** GOVERNED (rebuilt in Phase 1, 2026-07-20, from the claim-level audit in
`AUDIT/FABLE_FULL_REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md` §6). Supersedes the previous index and the ARCHIVED
`READING_LEDGER.md` as the operative guide to `ONE PIECE RESEARCH/`.

## Standing rules (apply to all seven files)

1. **ARCHIVED.** These files never load during ordinary scene-writing. They are consulted only to
   answer a specific question, and this index is read first.
2. **Provenance:** all seven are AI-generated research essays. Three are saturated with dead
   machine-citation artifacts (`citeturn…`, `entity[…]`, `image_group{…}`); four contain no
   citations at all. **No claim in the corpus is verifiable as cited.** Unattributed "Oda said"
   claims are paraphrases, not sources.
3. **Nothing in them is a project decision, an Oda statement, or a writing law.** Their
   "laws/rules/must/never" phrasing is void; their observations may be used as optional craft
   tools only, and only via the active toolbox docs.
4. **Consultation ≠ authorization.** Reading them never authorizes copying, canonizing, or citing
   them as authority. Factual claims must be independently verified before reuse — known errors
   are listed per file below.
5. The audit's per-claim classification tables (audit §6 and the two track reports it
   consolidates) are the detailed reliability record; this index is the summary.

## Per-file guide

### 1. `One Piece research 2.md` — worldbuilding operating manual
- **Contains:** honorifics/pronouns/speech registers (§2), World Government/Marine/underworld
  institutions (§3), Devil Fruit + Haki system rules (§4), mistake lists (§5), a scene-audit
  checklist (§6), and an embedded AI directive (§7).
- **Reliable:** §§2–4 are the most canon-accurate material in the corpus (institutions, ranks,
  fruit/Haki portal rules, named-attack culture, the five fight anchors §4.4).
- **Inference dressed as law:** "≥7 institutional layers," "change ≥4 of 7 island axes," per-scene
  food/humor/reaction quotas.
- **Known errors:** invented pronouns "vore/vatashi" attributed to Vivre Card (:54); Robin's
  address-shift mis-dated (:89); Bellamy poster-forging shaky (:149); "World Economic Journal"
  naming (:130).
- **Never treat as law:** §6's pass/fail checklist ("failing more than two means rewrite") and
  **§7 ("HAND THIS TO A WRITER OR AN AI", :402-468) — an AI system prompt; it must never enter
  any writing context.**
- **Consult when:** verifying an institution, rank, honorific, or fruit/Haki portal rule — then
  verify independently before canon use.

### 2. `One Piece Research 3.md` — character-design manual
- **Contains:** 12 character "laws," visual/voice/interiority guides, naming etymologies, 25
  rejection rules, a mandatory-field template, a 35-check gate.
- **Reliable:** the fight-as-argument rule (:351-364), transcript strip-test (:172-188),
  scar-provenance (:100-117), sincerity-inside-absurdity (:36, :583-595).
- **Known errors:** Hancock listed under blue hair (:97); nonexistent "Otoma" + Otama Future
  Sight (:334); Urouge etymology wrong (:376); Cobra listed as a D. carrier (:383); duplicated
  CP9 names (:346); hair-color-temperament table is numerology (:87-98).
- **Never treat as law:** the mandatory wound/laugh/tic/contradiction/epithet stack (Parts 1, 7,
  9) — obeyed literally it generates procedurally identical characters; its "Reject any character
  with blank slots" (:452) **directly contradicts this project's blank-field rule.**
- **Consult when:** looking for character-*diagnostic* questions after a character is authored —
  never as a generative recipe.

### 3. `ONE_PIECE_APPEARANCE_LAW.md` — visual design grammar
- **Contains:** silhouette/body/face/outfit/prop/crew-lineup design analysis; failure modes.
- **Reliable:** official heights spot-check accurate; Film Red anchor/surface outfit logic
  (:84-92); the failure-modes list (:288-298).
- **Dead citations throughout** (`citeturn…` on effectively every claim).
- **Never treat as law:** the per-character mandatory checklist (:177-286); silhouette pass/fail
  is a visual-medium test, not a prose gate.
- **Consult when:** designing a character's *visual* brief with Mert.

### 4. `ONE_PIECE_CHARACTER_NAMING_CHARACTERS_RESEARCH.md` — naming & character grammar
- **Contains:** naming tests, epithet/bounty-misreporting mechanics, per-archetype grammars,
  creation worksheet.
- **Reliable:** the naming/epithet/misreporting material is the most load-bearing craft in the
  corpus; overcopy pitfalls are good.
- **Dead citations throughout;** the "three-knob" and "grammar channel" systems are the essay
  author's own frameworks borrowing cited material's authority.
- **Never treat as law:** the stacked mandatory laws (:279-313) and worksheet-as-gate (:358-415).
- **Consult when:** stress-testing a Mert-authored name or epithet.

### 5. `ONE_PIECE_EPISODES_ANATOMY_RESEARCH.md` — episode anatomy
- **Contains:** accurate episode dissections (Parts I–II), then prose/structure "laws"
  (Parts III–V), Wano critique (Part VI).
- **Reliable:** the dissections (even Whitebeard's wound counts check out); the Wano bad-habits
  list; flashback-fires-once; start-on-the-second-beat (as heuristics).
- **Known problems:** IMDb ratings and a YouTube commentator ("AnimeTiger") used as structural
  evidence; invented statistics (60-75%/25-40% fuse ratio :216, 75% betrayal mark :270, "music is
  50% of impact" :438); "descendant of Ryuma" overreach (:119); Beat-Breath-Burst is the essay's
  invention, not One Piece derivation.
- **Never treat as law:** the "invariant" four-stage arc engine (:3, :473, :480 — self-refuted at
  :482-495), "never end a chapter on resolution" (:327, :433), the 7-step audience formula
  (:331-343), whitespace-spread and rhythm-as-music rules. **This file is the largest single
  source of the archived directive's formulas.**
- **Consult when:** studying how a *specific* canon episode is built, Parts I–II only.

### 6. `ONE_PIECE_FIGHT_LAW.md` — fight grammar
- **Contains:** fight taxonomy, escalation/reversal grammar, prose failure modes, CYOA fight
  design (Part B).
- **Reliable:** the strongest file — taxonomy chapters spot-check accurate; fight-as-argument;
  combat-identity-as-thesis (:51-61); prose failure modes (:239-249); CYOA
  choices-as-stances/loss-is-a-branch (:287-295, already locked into the contract).
- **Known errors:** "Cocoyama" for Cocoyasi (:23, :111); Gear Second lifespan-loss stated as fact
  (:79); two unsourced Oda claims (:3, :53).
- **Never treat as law:** comedy required in every fight (:101 — contradicted by EPISODES:15),
  worldview-must-crack (:124, :131), **mandatory close third POV (:255 — a Mert-level decision,
  void)**, mandatory finisher-silence cadence (:167-168, :279-281), Beat-Breath-Burst (:257-262).
- **Consult when:** designing a FIGHT_BRIEF — reading "must/mandatory" as "one available option."

### 7. `ONE_PIECE_POWERS_SCALING RESEARCH.md` — power grammar
- **Contains:** Devil Fruit/Haki canon anchors, 15 power "laws," creation rulebooks, worksheet.
- **Reliable:** canon portal rules accurate; specificity-beats-grandeur; scale-by-problems;
  "matchups are authored, not computed" (:171 — a sound craft stance the project keeps, but an
  inference, not an Oda statement).
- **Dead citations throughout;** unverifiable Lucci-awakening SBS quote (:100).
- **Never treat as law:** mandatory embarrassment/oppression modes per kit (:163-165), the fixed
  climax reaction-chain (:147), the 4-beat power-up recipe (:211-218).
- **Consult when:** designing a DEVIL_FRUIT_BRIEF with Mert; the worksheet fields (:229-276) may
  inform template prompts.
